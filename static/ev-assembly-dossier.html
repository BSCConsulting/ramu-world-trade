1. The Core Tariff Arbitrage (CBU vs. Component Import)

Your intuition is 100% commercially and statutorily correct. Under Indian Customs Tariff (First Schedule to Customs Tariff Act 1975) and Ministry of Heavy Industries Phased Manufacturing Programme (PMP):

  • Completely Built Unit (CBU - HS 8711.60): Attracts 50% to 100% BCD + 10% SWS + 18% IGST. Total effective landed duty exceeds 75%–135%! Importing ready-made scooters from China/Vietnam destroys your capital.
  • Individual Components / Parts Route:
    • BLDC Hub Motor (HS 8501.31): 7.5% - 10% BCD
    • Lithium-ion battery cells (HS 8507.60): 5% - 10% BCD
    • Motor Controller & Wiring Harness (HS 8504.40): 7.5% BCD
    • Alloy Bicycle Frame & Chassis (Ludhiana domestic sourcing): 0% Import Duty
    • Tires, Tubes & Rims (Domestic TVS/Ralco): 0% Import Duty

2. The Critical Legal Trap: Rule 2(a) "Essential Character" Seizure

Customs Tariff Interpretation Rule 2(a) Warning: If you order complete assembly kits from a single supplier in Ningbo or Shenzhen and pack matching quantities of motors, frames, controllers, and batteries into one container, Indian Customs will rule under Rule 2(a) of General Rules of Interpretation (GRI) that the unassembled components have the "essential character of a complete vehicle". They will reclassify the whole container as CBU, demand 100% duty, and levy penalties for misdeclaration!

The Legal Defense (Component Disaggregation):
1. Import only high-tech electronics (Motors, Controllers, Lithium Cells) from Shenzhen/Taiwan.
2. Procure structural chassis, cycle frames, forks, handlebars, and disc brakes domestically from India's bicycle manufacturing capital (Ludhiana, Punjab).
3. Source tires and wheels domestically from Indian manufacturers (CEAT/Ralco).
By integrating 45%+ domestic Indian mechanical parts, you completely defeat Rule 2(a) scrutiny!

3. The Regulatory Gateway: Low-Speed (<25 km/h) Exemption Moat

The Secret to Starting Without Millions in R&D:
• If an electric two-wheeler has a top speed of < 25 km/h and a motor rated ≤ 250 Watts, it is statutorily classified as a "Non-Motorised Vehicle" under Central Motor Vehicles Rules (CMVR) 1989.
• NO RTO Vehicle Registration required.
• NO Driving License required for customer.
• NO Road Tax required.
• NO expensive ₹25-Lakh ARAI crash testing required.
• You only need a one-time Type Exemption Test Certificate from ARAI / ICAT (cost: ~₹35,000) verifying speed < 25 km/h and 250W power limit. You can assemble, brand, and sell legally immediately!
