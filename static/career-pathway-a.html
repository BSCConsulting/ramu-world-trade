1. The Strategic Positioning: The "Found Money" Consultant

You never present yourself as an "unemployed candidate looking for a job" or a "traditional broker begging for business." You position yourself as an **External Supply-Chain & Statutory Incentive Auditor**. By combining your **Engineering background** (process optimization, quantitative modeling) and your **TISS Masters** (deep understanding of grassroots supply bases, FPOs, and regional producer networks), you bring a degree of analytical rigor that 95% of traditional commission brokers completely lack.

2. The Monetization Architecture

  • Monthly Retainer Model: Charge 4 to 6 mid-sized SME exporters ₹25,000 to ₹35,000/month to oversee their customs filing audit, freight forwarder quote verification, and incentive tracking. (4 clients @ ₹30k = **₹1,20,000/month baseline cash flow**).

  • Sourcing Commission: ₹2 to ₹4 per kg on verified agricultural/commodity lots (e.g., ₹35,000 per 14 MT container of chilli or spices).

3. The 3-Step Client Acquisition Process

Step 1 — The Pre-Flight Audit: Look up the target company on Zauba / Volza or DGCI&S shipping bill records. Identify their shipping ports (e.g. INNSA1 or INMAA1) and export commodities. Calculate their uncollected scrip spread or container dead-air waste.
Step 2 — The Executive Diagnostic Delivery: Send a 1-page "Diagnostic Cash Memo" directly to the Managing Director's WhatsApp/email showing them how your audit will save them ₹1.5 Lakhs per container.
Step 3 — The Retainer Closing: Meet the promoter. Offer: "Engage me for 60 days on a nominal retainer. If I do not uncover at least 3x my fee in statutory cash savings and lower freight, you owe me nothing." They cannot refuse this offer.
