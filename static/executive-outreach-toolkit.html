C-Suite Direct Access

1. How to Extract Real Contact Details (Promoter Mobiles & Emails)

  • MCA Director Master Data (Ministry of Corporate Affairs): Go to mca.gov.in ➔ Master Data ➔ View Company/LLP Master Data. Enter company name. Gives complete registered office address, company email, and Director DIN numbers. Search DIN on Zauba Corp or Tofler to get direct mobile numbers.
  • DGFT IEC Master Portal: Look up company IEC on dgft.gov.in. Displays registered port branch addresses and authorized signatory details.
  • Spices Board / EPC Registered Exporter Lists: Download official member directories (PDF/Excel) from Spices Board, AEPC, EEPC, or FIEO. These documents list direct proprietor mobile numbers and factory email IDs.
