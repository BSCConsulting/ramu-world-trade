1. Executive Concept & Corridor Dynamics

Operating a lean, rapid 14-hour cycle from Gollapudi Wholesale Market (Vijayawada, AP) to Madhira Town (Khammam District, Telangana). 3. Ground Politics, Syndicates & How to Win

  • Gollapudi 3:30 AM Entry Advantage: Never buy at 6:00 AM from secondary brokers. Arrive at 3:30 AM at the farmer arrival bays. Buy direct from the arriving tractor trailers ("Direct Rythu Malu") to avoid the 8% commission agent markup.
  • Inter-State Checkpost: Fresh vegetables are 100% exempt from GST under Notification No. 2/2017-Central Tax (Rate). Keep the white APMC Mandi receipt in the truck to easily cross the AP-Telangana border without border delays.
  • Local Street Politics in Madhira: Established cart vendors will complain if you park directly in front of their stalls. Counter-Strategy: Position your setup near high-traffic morning junctions (near Madhira Railway Station road or SBI branch junction) where office commuters and morning walkers buy quickly for convenience.

4. Licenses Required (Zero-Bureaucracy Setup)

1. FSSAI Registration: Basic petty food business registration online (₹100/yr via foscos.fssai.gov.in).
2. APMC Gollapudi Buyer Entry Token: Obtained at the gate counter daily (₹10).
3. Madhira Town Vending Pass: Daily municipality token ticket (₹30–₹50).
