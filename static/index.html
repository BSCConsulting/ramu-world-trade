Agricultural & Spices

Guntur Red Chilli JNPT Port Detention: Aflatoxin > 10 ppb Crisis

Consignment: ₹38,50,000 (20ft FCL • 14 MT) • Destination: Rotterdam, Netherlands
Quality & Customs Hold
1. The Ground Trigger:

Pre-shipment sampling by buyer nominated surveyor at JNPT CFS reported total Aflatoxin at 12.4 ppb, exceeding the EU statutory threshold of 10 ppb.

2. The Commercial Bottleneck:

Shipping line issued detention notice; container moved to CFS holding bay incurring ₹7,500/day ground rent and ₹12,000/day reefer plug-in fees. Buyer threatened to cancel LC.

3. Forensic Resolution Protocol:

1. Invoked Spices Board of India Mandatory Export Quality Sampling protocol under Spices Board Act 1986.

2. Commissioned emergency re-test with Vimta Labs NABL Accredited laboratory TC-5012 using HPLC-FLD fluorescence method.

3. Result confirmed batch split: 80% was sub-5 ppb, but outer 20 bags absorbed moisture during monsoon road haulage.

4. Executed on-dock de-stuffing, segregated contaminated bags, replaced with buffer inventory from cold storage in Navi Mumbai, and re-stuffed within 48 hours.

Statutory Legal Defense:

Spices Board Circular SEC/EXP/AFL/2023 & DGFT Notification No. 12/2015-2020: Proof of sorting and re-inspection certificate accepted for customs port clearance.

Financial Outcome: Demurrage capped at ₹39,000 (3 days). Saved ₹38.5L consignment from rejection.
