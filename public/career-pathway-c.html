1. The Vision: The Sovereign Agricultural Trading House

This is where your **TISS Rural Development education** and your **Engineering foundation** reach their zenith. Instead of acting as an employee or a lone broker, you establish an **ODOP Export Consortium**. You organize 50–100 micro-farmers or small village aggregators in your district into a unified export-grade supply stream, obtain an IEC code, and export under your own sovereign enterprise brand.

2. The 4-Pillar Aggregation Architecture

  • Pillar 1 — Rural Aggregation Depot: Contract a local packhouse / warehouse in Khammam or Guntur. Institute NABL quality pre-testing, grading, and batch QR code traceability.
  • Pillar 2 — Working Capital Float (TReDS & Packing Credit): Leverage your entity status to secure 75% Pre-Shipment Packing Credit under the RBI Master Direction with **3.0% MSME Interest Equalization Scheme (IES)**. Once the shipping bill is generated, discount invoices within 24 hours on the TReDS platform (RXIL / M1xchange) at 5% annualized interest.
  • Pillar 3 — Statutory Incentive Collection: Capture the full stack of government rebates: 3.9% RoSCTL/RoDTEP scrip + 3.2% Duty Drawback + Mandi cess exemptions.
  • Pillar 4 — Direct Buyer Supply: Sell directly to overseas buyers (Gulf supermarkets, European ingredient processors) on CIF/FOB contracts, securing 25%–35% gross profit margins.

3. The Progression Roadmap (A ➔ B ➔ C)

Phase 2 (Months 4–8 — Pathway B): Take on consulting retainers with 3 export houses or work in a strategic corporate capacity to master institutional financing, bank letters of credit (UCP 600), and foreign buyer negotiations.
