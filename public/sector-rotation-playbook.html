2. The 4-Week Agile Sector Rotation Sprint

Week 1 — Spices & Agro-Commodities (Guntur / Khammam Basin):
Focus: Teja Chilli, Turmeric, Cumin.
Action: Map 5 cold storages; audit 3 local spice exporters; study Spices Board CRES requirements.
Week 2 — Ready-Made Garments & Technical Textiles (Tirupur / Surat):
Focus: Knitted cotton shirts, industrial fabrics.
Action: Audit 5 apparel export houses on RoSCTL scrip monetization; test container CBM stuffing calculations.
Week 3 — Engineering Goods & Fasteners (Coimbatore / Rajkot):
Focus: Steel bolts, industrial valves, automotive forgings.
Action: Contact export managers regarding CBIC Section 12 landed duties and scrap import advance authorizations.
Week 4 — Marine Seafood or Handcrafted Artware:
Focus: Vannamei frozen shrimp (Bhimavaram) or Moradabad brassware.
Action: Evaluate cold-chain reefer demurrage rules vs high-margin handcrafted gift export channels.

3. The Sector Decision Filter (How to Pick Your Winning Sector)

At the end of the 4-week sprint, evaluate each sector against your 3 Personal Filters:
1. Physical Proximity: Can you physically visit the source within 2–3 hours? (Favors Agri/Spices in AP/Telangana).
2. Promoter Receptivity: Which promoters responded fastest to your Diagnostic Audit Memo?
3. Cash Velocity: Which sector settles receivables quickest without prolonged customs holds?
