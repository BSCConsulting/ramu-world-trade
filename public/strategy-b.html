3. Statutory & Regulatory Invariants

  • GST & Invoicing: Registered entity with active GSTIN. Since chilli is an agricultural item, sales to export houses are structured under 5% GST or under Rule 96A Letter of Undertaking (LUT) for zero-rated deemed exports.
  • Rule 138 e-Way Bill: Mandatory generation on ewaybillgst.gov.in with accurate HSN 0904.21.10, truck registration number, and destination CFS PIN code.
  • APMC Gate Pass: Ensure the cold storage owner provides the original Mandi Cess clearance certificate to prevent RTO vehicle seizure at inter-district border checkpoints.

4. Downside Risks & Defense Protocols

Risk #1 — Chemical Lab Rejection: If pesticide residues exceed EU MRLs (Maximum Residue Limits), the exporter will reject the cargo at the port. Defense: Never pay the storage owner the full amount without prior lab clearance.
Risk #2 — 45-Day Payment Freezes: Exporters often hold 20% retention until the container receives Let Export Order (LEO). Defense: Structure payment terms as 50% advance on loading, 40% against weighbridge slip, and 10% on port gate-in.
